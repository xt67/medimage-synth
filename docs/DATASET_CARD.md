## Preprocessing
- Resize 512×512 → 128×128 (bilinear, aspect ratio preserved by square resize).
- Grayscale, normalized to [-1, 1].
- Deduplication by MD5 before splitting.

## Limitations (to be reported in paper)
1. **No patient-level split** — images only; cannot guarantee patient
   disjointness. Potential for patient-level leakage if the same patient appears
   in multiple images. Documented as a limitation.
2. **Domain shift / source confounding** — Normal images come from RSNA (3094)
   and NLM (406); TB images from BELARUS (306) and NLM (394). A classifier
   may exploit source/dataset differences rather than TB pathology. This is a
   critical caveat; Grad-CAM and source-stratified analysis are recommended.
3. **Class imbalance** — 5:1 (3500:700) in raw; ~5:1 after dedup.
4. **Restricted subset** — only 700 of 3500 public TB images used; the
   remaining 2800 require an NIAID data-sharing agreement.
5. **Low resolution target** — 128×128 may not preserve fine clinical detail;
   visual similarity ≠ diagnostic fidelity.
6. **FID uses ImageNet Inception** — medical suitability documented separately.
